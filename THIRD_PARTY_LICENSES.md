## skills

https://github.com/mattpocock/skills

The way `/challenge` runs comes from `grilling`, and from `grill-me`, which calls it: a tree of decisions worked in rounds, numbered questions that each give a recommended answer and are worded so that "yes" accepts it, facts looked up and decisions left to the person, and the end when nothing is left silently assumed. Tailoring to a purpose, suggested skills, pointing at documents instead of copying them, and leaving out secrets come from `handoff`. All reworded and mixed with this mod's own text.
